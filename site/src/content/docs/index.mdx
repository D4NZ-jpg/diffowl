<div class="principle-row" aria-label="Diffowl review principles">
  <div>
    <strong>Evidence first</strong>
    <span>Findings carry repository evidence and explicit verification state.</span>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Policy from the base</strong>
    <span>Pull requests cannot weaken the rules used to review themselves.</span>
  </div>
  <div>
    <strong>Humans decide</strong>
    <span>Diffowl prepares the review. Your team makes the product decision.</span>
  </div>
</div>

<section class="home-section review-system">
  <div class="section-intro">
    <h2>One review. Three safeguards.</h2>
    <p>
      Diffowl separates generation, challenge, and verification so confident language never counts
      as proof by itself.
    </p>
  </div>

  <ol class="review-rail">
    <li>
      <strong>Classify</strong>
      <span>Diffowl classifies trust before it starts risky work.</span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Review</strong>
      <span>The reviewer grounds each candidate in the change and repository.</span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Challenge</strong>
      <span>The challenger rejects weak evidence and low-materiality claims.</span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Verify</strong>
      <span>Supported findings become a typed, auditable outcome.</span>
    </li>
  </ol>
</section>
